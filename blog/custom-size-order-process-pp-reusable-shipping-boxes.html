Custom Size Order Process for PP Reusable Shipping Boxes: From Spec to Delivery

Reusableshippingboxes Editorial • September 2026

Understanding Custom Sizing Requirements and Design Constraints

Standard reusable shipping boxes rarely fit specialized packaging workflows. Custom sizing allows procurement teams to optimize container dimensions for specific product SKUs, pallet configurations, or automated handling systems. Before initiating a custom order, define your dimensional requirements by measuring actual product dimensions, considering stacking height limits, and calculating pallet utilization efficiency. A properly sized reusable container maximizes cube utilization, reduces void fill, and improves warehouse density.

Polypropylene corrugated boxes offer design flexibility within manufacturing constraints. Twin-wall PP sheets typically come in 2mm to 10mm thicknesses, with 4mm and 6mm being the most common for reusable shipping applications. When working with reusable storage and shipping boxes from Elipacko, you can specify internal dimensions, external dimensions, or both, and the engineering team will recommend appropriate material gauge based on load requirements and stacking cycles. Wall thickness directly affects rigidity, load rating, and cost per unit.

Initial Consultation and Technical Feasibility Review

The custom order process begins with a technical consultation to validate that your specifications are manufacturable and cost-effective. Key discussion points include:

  • Dimensional limits — maximum sheet size constraints, corner radius minimums, and height-to-width ratio stability
  • Load capacity requirements — static load, dynamic load, and stacking load based on your distribution cycle
  • Material selection — virgin vs. recycled resin, FDA food-grade certification, UV stabilization for outdoor storage
  • Feature integration — handles, label pockets, ventilation slots, collapsibility, and interlocking mechanisms
  • Finish options — natural, white, or custom colors; surface treatments for printing or labeling

Elipacko's engineering team conducts a feasibility review within 48 hours of receiving initial specifications. The review identifies any design modifications needed for structural integrity, manufacturing efficiency, or cost optimization. For example, adjusting a dimension by 10mm might allow nesting on standard pallet footprints or enable more efficient sheet utilization during fabrication, reducing material waste and unit cost.

Prototyping, Testing, and Design Validation

After feasibility approval, the next phase is prototype development. For reusable PP corrugated boxes, prototyping involves cutting and assembling samples from production-grade material using the proposed fabrication method. Physical prototypes allow you to validate fit with actual products, test handling ergonomics, and confirm compatibility with existing warehouse equipment like conveyors, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), or robotic pick systems.

Testing protocols for custom reusable boxes should include:

  • Load testing — place maximum anticipated load in the box and stack to expected heights; measure deflection and check for stress cracking
  • Drop testing — simulate handling abuse by dropping loaded prototypes from typical handling heights (waist height, forklift discharge)
  • Cycle testing — repeatedly load, stack, transport, and empty the box to simulate 50-100 use cycles
  • Cleaning validation — run prototypes through your planned sanitation process to confirm material compatibility and dimensional stability

Elipacko provides 3-5 prototype samples at no cost for orders exceeding minimum quantities. Customers conduct on-site validation testing, and the engineering team adjusts designs based on feedback. Typical design iteration cycles take 5-7 business days per round. This validation phase prevents costly tooling investments in designs that fail operational requirements.

Tooling, Molds, and Setup Costs

Custom PP corrugated boxes require fabrication tooling—cutting dies, scoring fixtures, and assembly jigs. Unlike injection molding, which involves expensive molds costing $10,000-$50,000, corrugated plastic tooling is relatively affordable. Cutting and scoring dies for custom box blanks typically range from $800 to $2,500 depending on complexity, size, and feature count. Corner assembly fixtures and ultrasonic welding jigs add another $500-$1,500.

Total tooling investment for a custom reusable shipping box averages $1,500 to $4,000. Tooling is amortized over the initial production run, so unit cost decreases as order quantities increase. Elipacko owns tooling on behalf of customers, storing it for reorder runs without additional setup charges. Tooling life typically exceeds 100,000 units, making it a one-time investment for long-term reusable packaging programs.

Lead time for tooling fabrication is 2-3 weeks after design finalization. Production can begin immediately upon tooling completion, with first article inspection confirming dimensional accuracy before full production runs.

Minimum Order Quantities and Pricing Structure

MOQ for custom PP reusable shipping boxes depends on tooling amortization and production efficiency. Standard MOQs range from 500 to 1,000 units for simple designs, and 1,000 to 2,500 units for complex boxes with multiple features. Lower MOQs are available but result in higher per-unit costs due to tooling allocation.

Pricing structure includes three components:

  • Tooling cost — one-time charge for cutting dies, scoring fixtures, and assembly jigs
  • Material cost — based on PP sheet area, gauge thickness, and resin grade (virgin FDA vs. industrial recycled)
  • Fabrication cost — cutting, scoring, corner welding, and assembly labor

Volume pricing tiers typically offer 8-12% cost reduction at 2,500+ units, 15-20% at 5,000+ units, and 25-30% at 10,000+ units. Elipacko sources FDA-approved virgin PP resin from Thailand and Vietnam with zero antidumping duties, providing cost advantages over domestic or Chinese-manufactured alternatives. The company's planned US manufacturing facility will offer domestic production options for customers requiring 100% US-made packaging.

Production Lead Times and Delivery Logistics

After tooling completion and first article approval, production lead times for custom reusable boxes vary by order quantity:

  • 500-1,000 units: 3-4 weeks
  • 1,000-2,500 units: 4-5 weeks
  • 2,500-5,000 units: 5-6 weeks
  • 5,000+ units: 6-8 weeks

These timelines assume overseas manufacturing in Thailand or Vietnam. Shipping via ocean freight adds 4-6 weeks to US West Coast ports, 6-8 weeks to East Coast. Air freight is available for urgent orders, reducing transit to 5-7 days at 3-4x ocean freight cost.

Elipacko coordinates freight forwarding, customs clearance, and final-mile delivery to customer distribution centers. Containers are palletized for forklift unloading, with units nested or stacked to maximize container utilization. For ongoing reusable packaging programs, establish blanket purchase orders with scheduled releases to maintain inventory levels without large upfront capital commitment.

Reorder Process and Design Modifications

Once tooling is complete and initial production validated, reorders follow a simplified process. Elipacko stores customer tooling and maintains design specifications in a digital library, enabling quick-turn reorders without re-engineering. Typical reorder lead times drop to 3-5 weeks plus freight, as tooling setup is eliminated.

Design modifications for existing tooling are possible within limits. Adding or relocating label pockets, adjusting handle positions, or changing surface finish can often be accomplished without new tooling. Dimensional changes exceeding 5% or structural alterations (changing wall thickness, corner reinforcement) require new tooling investment. Procurement teams should plan for 18-24 months of use before considering design revisions, allowing sufficient cycles to identify real-world performance issues worth engineering changes.
